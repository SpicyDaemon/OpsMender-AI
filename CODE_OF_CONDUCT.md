# Code of Conduct

## Our goal

AI Incident Manager is an open project and we want it to stay a productive, friendly place to collaborate. This document describes the behavior we expect from everyone who participates — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other project space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in all interactions. Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback focused on the code, the design, or the idea — not the person.
- Accept that reasonable people disagree, and that the maintainers make the final call on scope and direction.
- When you are reviewing someone's work, remember they took the time to contribute.
- When you are receiving review, remember the reviewer is trying to help.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind
- Sharing someone else's private information without their permission
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or repeated derailment of technical discussions
- Deliberate misuse of the project (for example, filing AI-generated issues with no verification, or using the project to target or harm others)

## Reporting

If you see behavior that breaks this code of conduct, contact the maintainers privately at **noreply@opsmender.local**. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may, at their discretion, edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and ban contributors who violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations will result in a permanent ban from the project.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies inside the project's GitHub repository, any official chat channels, and any public space where someone is representing the project.
